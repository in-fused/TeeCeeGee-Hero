# PackFinder Network — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Problem Statement
Collectors and retailers lack a reliable way to determine where high-demand Pokémon and One Piece sealed products
(ETBs, booster boxes, ENG products) are physically available or inbound.

## Goals
- ZIP-based discovery (5 / 15 / 20 mile radius)
- Multi-retailer support (big box, LGS, vending)
- Real delivery & shipment signal ingestion
- Explicit data provenance and trust labeling
- No mock or fabricated data
